Contract rate checker

How the call is made

Your cost per route day includes the weekly claims and fees, spread across your route days. If the margin clears your target, it's a go. If it's under your target but above your walk-away line, it's caution. Below the walk-away line, it's a no-go.

The rates that matter are worked out from your own costs: break-even (no profit), walk-away, and target. If you're paid per stop, they're shown per stop at your average stops.

Escrow isn't counted as a cost because it's still your money. It's shown separately because it's cash you can't use for payroll or fuel.

Before you sign

  • Get the rate, the chargeback rules, and the escrow refund terms in writing.
  • Price any extra insurance the contract requires before you agree to it.
  • Have an attorney read anything you're unsure about. This tool isn't legal advice.

Questions

What margin should I target?

That's your call, but leave room for a bad week. The checker starts at a 25% target and a 15% walk-away line. Change them to match your business.

Why compare with my current routes?

It shows whether this offer is better or worse than the work you already have, which is often the real decision.

Does it account for slow pay?

Not in the profit math. If they pay slower than weekly, check that term so it lands on your push-back list, and plan your cash.

Is anything I enter saved or sent anywhere?

No. It all runs in your browser and is remembered only on this device.
